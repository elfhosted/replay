                    Who can see your activity

                    Sync only works if the record lives on a server, so all three keep the same kinds of entry. The difference is whose server it is, and who there can read it.

                    Stremio Stremio’s servers Readable by their staff and infrastructure.
                    Nuvio NuvioMedia’s servers Readable by their staff and infrastructure.
                    A Nuvio backend you run Yours Readable by you, and by whoever hosts it.

                    Stremio has no backend you can run yourself, so moving means changing app as well as server: only Nuvio's apps can be pointed at one. That backend does store more than Stremio's, because it also holds your profiles, your devices and your provider keys, which is how your apps set themselves up the moment you sign in. Whoever runs the server can read it, and that includes us if you have us run it. What changes is that you pick who, your record sits in its own database rather than pooled with everyone else's, and you can export the lot and take it elsewhere whenever you like.

                    Whether any of that matters is your call. Plenty of people read this and shrug, which is a fine answer.

                    Don't like the answer?

                    Take a copy first. RePlay already has it, so it is one click.

                    RePlay can write that file back to Stremio, or to a backend you run. Nuvio's own backend will hand you a copy but will not take one back, so there it is a record rather than a rescue.

                    • Clear the playback data. Wipes the positions, dates and watch counts for whichever period you choose. Your library and addons are untouched.

                      It does not remove the titles. Stremio can read entries and write entries, and that is all it can do, so a title stays in your account with nothing recorded against it. Restoring your copy undoes this.                       Delete the playback entries. Removes them outright rather than blanking them, for whichever period you choose. Your library, addons and profiles are untouched.

                      This one cannot be undone on a hosted Nuvio account, because it will not accept a restore. On a backend you run yourself it can.

                    • Delete the account. The only way to remove the titles as well. It lives in your app under Settings, and it takes the whole log with it.
                    • Keep it somewhere of your own. The only option where you can remove a single entry and put it back. Delete the backend later and its backups go too, because they only ever held your data. On a shared one that is not possible, since your rows sit in the same backup files as everybody else's.
